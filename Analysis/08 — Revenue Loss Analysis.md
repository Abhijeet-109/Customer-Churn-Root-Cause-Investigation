# 08 — Revenue Loss Analysis

## Objective

The revenue impact of customer churn was estimated using the `Estimated_Revenue_Loss_INR` field available in the dataset.

The analysis focuses on the financial impact of the increase in churn during the six-month observation period.

---

## Overall Revenue Impact

During the analysis period:

- **Total customers:** 12,060
- **Churned customers:** 1,535
- **Overall churn rate:** 12.73%
- **Estimated revenue loss:** **₹99.67 lakh**

The estimated loss represents revenue associated with customers recorded as churned in the dataset.

---

## Monthly Revenue Loss

| Month | Churned Customers | Churn Rate | Estimated Revenue Loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mar 2026 | 139 | 7.0% | ₹8.74 lakh |
| Apr 2026 | 200 | 9.9% | ₹11.64 lakh |
| May 2026 | 250 | 12.0% | ₹15.00 lakh |
| Jun 2026 | 286 | 14.3% | ₹19.72 lakh |
| Jul 2026 | 304 | 15.3% | ₹20.73 lakh |
| Aug 2026 | 356 | 18.1% | ₹23.84 lakh |

Revenue loss increased alongside the rise in churn.

From March to August, monthly churn increased from **7.0% to 18.1%**, while estimated monthly revenue loss increased from **₹8.74 lakh to ₹23.84 lakh**.

---

## Revenue Loss by Churn Reason

The financial impact was further examined across the major recorded churn reasons.

| Churn Reason | Business Impact |
|---|---|
| Service Quality | Highest priority due to large churn contribution |
| Pricing | Significant revenue exposure |
| Product Issues | Significant customer and revenue impact |
| Competition | Lower overall volume but important for high-exposure customers |

Service quality, pricing, and product issues represent the main areas requiring financial attention because of their contribution to customer churn.

---

## Revenue Risk

The increase in churn represents more than a customer-retention problem. Continued churn at the current level can create a substantially higher recurring revenue impact compared with the earlier 8% churn baseline.

The August result of **18.1% churn** is particularly significant because it represents more than twice the March churn rate of **7.0%**.

---

## Business Interpretation

The analysis shows a clear financial consequence of the churn increase:

> **As customer churn increased during the six-month period, the associated monthly revenue loss also increased substantially.**

The estimated **₹99.67 lakh** revenue loss across churned customers highlights the need to prioritize the highest-impact churn drivers identified in the root cause analysis.

Retention efforts should therefore focus first on **service quality and product issues**, while targeted pricing and competitive-retention measures should address additional high-risk customers.
