# 03 — Customer Churn Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to measure customer churn and understand how it changed during the six-month period in which churn increased from **8% to 18%**.

The analysis focuses on the overall churn trend and the difference between churned and retained customers.

---

## Key Metrics

The following metrics will be used:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Retained Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Change in Churn Rate

### Churn Rate

```text
Churn Rate = Churned Customers / Total Customers × 100
```

### Retention Rate

```text
Retention Rate = Retained Customers / Total Customers × 100
```

---

## Churn Trend

Churn will be analyzed across the available time periods to identify:

- Starting churn rate
- Current churn rate
- Monthly changes
- Largest increase in churn
- Periods with unusually high churn

The main comparison will be between the period around the **8% baseline** and the period where churn reached approximately **18%**.

---

## Churned vs Retained Customers

Customer characteristics will be compared between churned and retained customers.

Areas of comparison include:

- Tenure
- Contract type
- Monthly charges
- Product/service usage
- Payment method
- Support interactions
- Complaints
- Service quality indicators
- Competitive factors

This comparison will help identify characteristics that are more common among customers who leave.

---

## Churn Analysis Questions

The analysis will answer:

1. What is the overall churn rate?
2. How has churn changed over time?
3. When was the largest increase observed?
4. Which customer characteristics differ most between churned and retained customers?
5. Are the changes concentrated in specific customer groups?
6. Which factors should be investigated further as possible churn drivers?

---

## Power BI Analysis

The following visuals will be used where relevant:

- Monthly churn trend
- Churned vs retained customer count
- Churn rate by major customer attributes
- Churn rate comparison across periods
- KPI cards for overall churn metrics

The final visual selection will depend on the findings from the dataset.

---

## Interpretation

The purpose of this stage is to identify **where and when the churn problem is occurring**.

The results from this analysis will provide the basis for the next stages:

- Customer segment analysis
- Hypothesis validation
- Root cause investigation
- Revenue impact analysis
