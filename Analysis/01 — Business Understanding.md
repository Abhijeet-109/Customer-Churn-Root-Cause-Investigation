# 01 — Business Understanding

## Problem Statement

Customer churn has increased significantly from **8% to 18% over the last six months**.

The purpose of this analysis is to understand what contributed to this increase, identify the customer groups most affected, and estimate the business impact of the additional churn.

The investigation will mainly focus on four possible areas mentioned in the assessment:

- Pricing
- Product / service issues
- Competition
- Service quality

Other relevant factors found in the data will also be considered.

---

## Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Measure the change in customer churn over the analysis period.
- Identify when the increase in churn occurred.
- Find customer segments with higher churn.
- Compare customer characteristics between churned and retained customers.
- Investigate possible reasons for the increase in churn.
- Form and validate hypotheses using the available data.
- Rank the major contributing factors by their impact.
- Estimate the revenue loss associated with churn.
- Provide practical recommendations to reduce future churn.

---

## Key Questions

### Churn

- How has churn changed over the last six months?
- Which period experienced the largest increase?
- How does the current churn rate compare with the previous level of 8%?

### Customer Segments

- Which customer segments have the highest churn?
- Which segments experienced the largest increase?
- Are particular customer characteristics associated with higher churn?

### Possible Causes

The following hypotheses will be investigated:

| Hypothesis        | Question                                                    |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pricing           | Are customers facing higher charges more likely to churn?   |
| Product / Service | Are product or service issues associated with higher churn? |
| Competition       | Is competitive pressure associated with customer churn?     |
| Service Quality   | Do poor support or service experiences contribute to churn? |

These will be treated as **hypotheses**, not assumptions. The final conclusion will depend on the evidence available in the dataset.

---

## Key Metrics

The analysis will track:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Monthly Churn Trend
- Churn by Customer Segment
- Revenue from Churned Customers
- Estimated Revenue Loss

Additional metrics may be added if required by the data.

---

## Analysis Approach

The investigation will follow this sequence:

```text
Data Audit
    ↓
Data Cleaning
    ↓
Churn Trend Analysis
    ↓
Segment Analysis
    ↓
Hypothesis Testing
    ↓
Root Cause Analysis
    ↓
Impact Ranking
    ↓
Revenue Loss
    ↓
Recommendations
```

The analysis will be performed using **Power Query and Power BI/DAX**.

---

## Expected Outcome

The final analysis should provide a data-supported explanation of:

1. Why churn increased from 8% to 18%.
2. Which customer segments were most affected.
3. Which factors have the strongest evidence of contributing to churn.
4. Which root causes have the greatest business impact.
5. How much revenue is associated with the increased churn.
6. What actions should be prioritized to reduce future churn.
